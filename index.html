Principles

What actually gets you shut down — and how we fight it.

01

No platform dependency

Your site lives on infrastructure you control — not rented land on a platform that can change its rules overnight. You own the domain, the content, the data.

02

Privacy by default

WHOIS privacy, discreet billing, optional pseudonyms, no third-party trackers unless you ask. Your clients' data stays yours.

03

Accessibility is non-negotiable

Keyboard navigation, colour contrast, semantic HTML, screen-reader support. Your site works for everyone — no exceptions.

04

Consent & age-gating

Built-in age verification gates, consent-forward UX patterns, clear content warnings. Tools that keep you compliant without being awkward.

05

Community over competition

Sliding scale, pay-it-forward, mutual aid invoicing. If you can pay more, you keep the doors open for someone who can't.

06

Education as empowerment

Every project includes training. You'll know how to update your own site, manage your list, and troubleshoot before you need us.
