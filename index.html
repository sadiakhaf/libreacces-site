The risk

What gets people shut down?

It isn't one company being cruel. It's a chain of policies, from payment law to platform terms, built to push adult businesses off the internet.

Payment processors

Stripe, PayPal, Square, and most banks treat adult income as high-risk. One flagged transaction can freeze revenue. FOSTA-SESTA-style laws made platforms afraid of anything that looks like sex work.

Web hosts & registrars

Mainstream hosts and domain registrars enforce vague "acceptable use" clauses. Kink, sex work, or even adjacent content can be removed mid-billing cycle, often without appeal.

Social & ad platforms

Instagram, TikTok, Google Ads, and app stores shadowban, demonetize, or delete accounts when advertisers or algorithmic reviewers complain. The rules change overnight.

Privacy exposure

Doxxing, WHOIS leaks, and billing records can out workers and clients. A site that isn't built for discretion can expose real identities.

The countermove

Ownership is the only real defense.

A domain you control, care that tolerates adult business, payment processors that don't moralize, and privacy-by-default design are the pieces that keep you online when platforms say no. We build that stack.

How we work

We build the skeleton. You learn it.

We don't just deliver a site. We set up the structure. Domain, data, backups, decision-making. So you can stand on your own if a platform changes the rules.

Start with ownership

Domain, care plan, backups, and data stay under your control from day one. If a provider boots you, the site can move. No lock-in.

Protect by default

WHOIS privacy, discreet billing, age-gating, consent tools, and no third-party trackers unless you ask. Safety is built in, not bolted on.

Teach as we build

Every project includes plain-language training. You learn how to update your site, read alerts, and respond to platform changes without waiting for rescue.

Price with dignity

Sliding scale: pay what fits your month, no proof required. If you can pay forward, you fund someone else's access rate.
